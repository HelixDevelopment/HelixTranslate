Technical Requirements

Video Quality Standards

Resolution: 1920x1080 (Full HD) minimum
Frame Rate: 30fps
Audio: 48kHz, 16-bit, minimum 320kbps
Bitrate: 8-12 Mbps (1080p)
Format: MP4 (H.264)

Recording Setup

│   ├── Sample Rate: 48 kHz
│   ├── Channels: Stereo
│   └── Audio Bitrate: 320 kbps

│   ├── Format: MP4
